twig is a lightweight, tab-heavy browser for macOS on Apple silicon. Tabs you aren't looking at fall asleep and cost almost nothing, and the pages you've read stay findable, even after you close them.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/docs/getting-started): install twig, get past the first launch, and take the welcome tour.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts): every default, and how to change them.
- [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting): Gatekeeper, default browser, sign-in pop-ups.

## What twig does

| Feature | In short |
| --- | --- |
| [Sleeping tabs](/docs/tabs) | Idle tabs give back their memory and wake where you left them |
| [Spaces and split view](/docs/spaces) | Separate piles of tabs, two pages side by side |
| [Recall](/docs/recall) | Full-text search of pages you've read, stored on your Mac |
| [Changes since you last read](/docs/changes) | A chip and a panel that show what's different on a page |
| [Checkpoints](/docs/checkpoints) | Named snapshots of a space you can resume or fork |
| [Snooze and sweep](/docs/snooze) | Put tabs away until later; tidy up ones you haven't touched |
| [Research packages](/docs/research-packages) | Bundle a space's sources, notes and excerpts into one file |
| [Command palette](/docs/command-palette) | ⌘K for tabs, bookmarks, history, pages you've read and commands |
| [Reader view and link hints](/docs/reading) | Read cleanly; follow links without the mouse |

Everything twig stores stays on your Mac. See [Privacy and your data](/docs/privacy).
