title: Privacy and your data
description: What twig stores, where it lives, what leaves your Mac, and how to delete it.
---

twig has no account, no sync server, no telemetry and no analytics. What you browse stays on your Mac.

## What's stored, and where

twig keeps its data in its app data folder, `~/Library/Application Support/com.twig.browser/`, and in macOS's WebKit storage.

| Data | Stored |
| --- | --- |
| History, bookmarks, closed tabs, snoozed tabs | A local SQLite database (`twig.db`) |
| Recall's reading copies and full-text index | The same database (SQLite FTS5) |
| Checkpoints | On your Mac |
| Research package library | `research-packages.json`, on your Mac |
| Tabs and spaces (for session restore) | On your Mac |
| Cookies, site storage, sign-ins | macOS's WebKit storage, on your Mac |
| Settings and shortcuts | On your Mac |

## Private windows

**⇧⌘N** opens a private window with a non-persistent store. Nothing it visits is added to history, indexed by Recall, captured in a checkpoint, or kept as cookies after you close it.

## What leaves your Mac

- **Websites you visit**, as with any browser.
- **Your search engine** gets what you search for (Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave or Bing, your choice).
- **Bug reports, only if you send one.** The Report a Bug form sends what you write (and, if you leave the box ticked, the twig and macOS versions) to a relay that files it as a public GitHub issue. An optional screenshot is stored publicly too. See [Reporting bugs](/docs/reporting-bugs).

Exported research packages and Markdown or HTML files only go where you send them.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Data → Clear history** clears history and Recall's reading copies.
- **Settings → Data → Clear site data** clears cookies and site storage.
- In [Recall](/docs/recall), delete a single copy or forget every copy of a page.
- Delete individual checkpoints and packages from their panels.

To remove everything, quit twig and delete `~/Library/Application Support/com.twig.browser/`, then move twig to the Bin.
