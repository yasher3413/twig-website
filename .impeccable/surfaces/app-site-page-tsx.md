# Home (/) surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Apple Silicon Mac users who hoard tabs (devs, researchers, students, writers), sceptical of new browsers. Action: Download for Mac. Proof: live, working recreations of twig's own UI; true facts (3.8 MB DMG, v0.1.3, MIT). Constraints: no invented numbers or social proof; demo values labelled; WCAG 2.2 AA; LCP < 2 s.

## Direction contract

THESIS: twig is a browser about time, so the page is an almanac of what sleeps, wakes and returns, and when. It refuses the category default of a centred serif headline over a big product window on cream with feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: flat lithographic colour fields in the app's six accents (Moss, Ocean, Clay, Honey, Plum, Slate) own whole chapters; paper between them; hairline almanac rules and tabular-figure time columns; Young Serif display, Public Sans text; twig's real chrome at true scale with the app's shadows; a moss branch growing down the margin; two forest-night chapters lit by one light.

STORY: the visitor sees twig's tabs fall asleep on their own, understands that tabs cost nothing, then tries each mechanism (wake, recall, changes, snooze, fork, ⌘K, keys), learns it's local and light, and downloads, knowing the Open Anyway step in advance.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left 5/12 on paper: a date-column "Today" beside a three-line Young Serif headline (~128px at 1440) "Every other browser forgets. twig remembers.", one sentence, then Download for Mac (moss, magnetic, v0.1.3 · Apple Silicon chip) and "See what's new". Right 7/12: a moss field bleeding to the edge holding the living twig window (~760×480) with leaf light behind; its tabs settle asleep along a printed 0/5/10-minute ruler while the memory ledger changes in place. The branch starts at the window and grows down.

FORM: The Gardener's Almanac, candidate 5 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key e2ad5003.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the branch that sleeps as you read (leaves unfurl on arrival, settle pale and curled once passed, wake on return; forks with the checkpoints demo).
Motion grammar: grow / unfurl / settle / drift easings; one authored moment per chapter; reduced motion = composed end frames.
