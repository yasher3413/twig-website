title: Reader view and link hints
description: Read a page without the clutter, follow links without the mouse, and the other reading tools.
---

## Reader view

Press **⇧⌘R** to strip a page down to its article. Reader view is a heuristic and will occasionally pick the wrong block of a page; press it again to return.

## Link hints

Press **⌘E** and twig labels every link on screen. Type a label to follow that link, without reaching for the mouse.

## Find in page

**⌘F** searches the current page.

## Zoom

**⌘+**, **⌘-** and **⌘0** zoom in, out and reset. Zoom is remembered per site, across restarts.

## Downloads

Downloads are saved to your Downloads folder without overwriting existing files. twig shows a notification when one finishes; there isn't a full download manager yet.

## Omnibox autofill

The address bar completes sites, not old searches: type `git` and it offers `github.com`, highlighted so your next letter replaces it. Press Tab or Enter to take it. Choose your search engine in Settings: Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave or Bing.
