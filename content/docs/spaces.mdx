## Spaces

A space is a separate pile of tabs: one for work, one for a trip, one for something you're researching. Switch spaces from the space menu in the toolbar. Each space keeps its own tab order.

## Split view

Split view shows two pages side by side in one window. Use the split button on a tab; the split partner is marked with an underline in the tab strip.

## Session restore

Tabs and spaces survive a quit. When you open twig again, everything is where you left it, and restored tabs stay asleep until you open them, so restoring costs almost nothing.

## Private windows

**⇧⌘N** opens a private window. It uses a non-persistent store: nothing it visits is saved to history, indexed by Recall, captured in checkpoints, or kept as cookies after you close it. Private windows can't snooze tabs or make research packages.
