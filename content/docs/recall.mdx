---
title: Recall
description: Search the text of pages you've actually read, by the words you remember, and read the saved copy even if the page changes.
---

Recall searches plain-text reading copies of pages you visited in a regular twig window. Open it with **⇧⌘F**, the toolbar's Recall button, the new tab page, or ⌘K.

## Searching

Type words you remember. Search uses SQLite FTS5 word matching with English stemming; it doesn't interpret natural-language dates. Narrow results by **space** and by **calendar dates**: the end date includes the whole day, in your time zone. Recall shows up to 100 matching copies, so narrower dates and words help with older ones.

## Reading and reopening

Select a result to read its saved plain text, with your words marked. No website loads to show a saved copy, and copies contain text only: no images, scripts, cookies, form state or page markup.

**Open live page at passage** opens a new tab and tries to select the passage once the site loads. It tries up to 12 times over about six seconds; redirects, rewritten text and dynamic layouts can stop it finding the spot. Your saved copy stays available either way, and your original tabs aren't replaced.

**Captured around then** lists up to eight other pages from the same space and browsing session, within 30 minutes of the selected copy. It's time context, not a guess about relevance.

## What's captured

When a page loads, twig captures up to **40,000 characters** of its rendered text, from HTTP(S) pages without embedded credentials. Capture skips forms, editable documents and controls, scripts, styles, hidden elements, and private windows.

Very large pages, embedded frames, shadow DOM and content loaded later may be captured incompletely. Recall is a reading aid, not a full archive of a website. Text you can see while signed in can be captured, so use a private window when you don't want page text saved.

## Limits

- Up to **2,000** saved copies. When full, new captures pause (Recall shows a notice) and existing copies stay; nothing is evicted automatically.
- An identical address, title and text in the same space within 15 minutes counts as a duplicate, and keeps its original time.
- Changed versions of a page are kept separately, each with its own date.

## Forgetting

- **Delete this copy** removes only the selected dated copy.
- **Forget this page** removes every saved copy of that exact address.
- Deleting a history entry also removes all reading copies for its address.
- **Settings → Data → Clear history** clears history and Recall copies together.

Deletion updates the search index and rejects late captures from before it, so a pending capture can't bring forgotten text back. This is deletion from SQLite, not forensic erasure. Open tabs, bookmarks, checkpoints and saved research packages are kept.
