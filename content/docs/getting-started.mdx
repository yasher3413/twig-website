## What you need

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). There's no Intel, Windows or Linux build.

## Install

1. [Download twig](https://github.com/yasher3413/twig/releases/latest/download/twig-macos-arm64.dmg). The link always serves the newest version.
2. Open the `.dmg` and drag **twig** into **Applications**.

## The first launch

twig isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it. You only do this once.

1. Open twig. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to the message about twig. Confirm with your password.

After that, twig opens normally. If you'd rather use Terminal, this does the same thing:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/twig.app
```

<Callout title="Why the extra step?">
Notarization needs a paid Apple Developer account. Until twig has one, Gatekeeper treats it as an app from an unidentified developer. [Supporting twig](/donate) would pay for it.
</Callout>

## The welcome tour

The first launch opens a short welcome. Every step is skippable, and nothing in it creates an account or leaves your Mac.

- **A name** for the new tab page to greet you by.
- **Bookmark import** from Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi or Chromium. Safari isn't in the list because its bookmarks need Full Disk Access.
- **A look**: light, dark or system, and one of six accents (Moss, Clay, Ocean, Plum, Honey, Slate).
- **How many tabs stay awake**: Frugal 3, Balanced 5 or Roomy 10.
- **Your shortcuts**, which you can rebind right there.

You can replay it any time from Settings or ⌘K → *Welcome to twig*.

## Make twig your default browser

Open **Settings → Browser** and choose to make twig your default. Links from other apps then open as new tabs in your current space, and sign-in pop-ups work.

## Updating

There's no auto-updater yet. When a new version comes out, download it from the same link and replace the app in Applications. The [release notes](/releases) list what's new, and have an [Atom feed](/releases/feed.xml).
